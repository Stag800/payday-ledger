PAYDAY LEDGER: SETUP GUIDE
==========================

You will do three things, once:
  A. Put the app online for free with GitHub Pages (about 5 minutes, on your computer)
  B. Turn on sync with your own free Firebase project (about 10 minutes, on your computer)
  C. Install it on your Android phone and your computer (2 minutes)

After that, you open Payday Ledger from its own icon. No Claude needed.


A. PUT THE APP ONLINE (GitHub Pages, free)
------------------------------------------
1. Unzip payday-ledger.zip on your computer. You get a folder with index.html,
   sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, four icon images and this guide.
2. Create a free account at https://github.com (skip this if you have one).
3. Click the + button at the top right, then "New repository".
   - Repository name: payday-ledger
   - Leave it Public (your budget data is NOT stored here, only the app's code)
   - Click "Create repository".
4. On the new, empty repository page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
5. Open the unzipped folder, select ALL the files inside it, and drag them onto the
   GitHub page. Wait for them to upload, then click "Commit changes".
6. Click the "Settings" tab of the repository, then "Pages" in the left menu.
   Under "Build and deployment":
   - Source: Deploy from a branch
   - Branch: main, folder: / (root)
   - Click Save.
7. Wait 1 to 2 minutes and refresh that page. It shows your address, like:
       https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/payday-ledger/
   Open it in Chrome. That is your app.


B. TURN ON SYNC (Firebase, free)
--------------------------------
In the app on your computer, go to Settings and tap "Set up sync".
The app walks you through these steps and shows the exact text to copy:
1. Create a project at https://console.firebase.google.com
2. Authentication: Get started, then turn on Email/Password.
3. Authentication, Settings, Authorized domains: add YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io
4. Firestore: Create database (Standard edition, a nearby location, production mode).
5. Firestore, Rules tab: paste the rules the app shows, then Publish.
6. Project settings, Your apps, the </> (web) button: register the app and copy
   the firebaseConfig block into the app.
Then create your sync email and password in the app. Anything you already entered
on that device moves into sync automatically.

The free Firebase plan (Spark) is far more than one person's budget needs.


C. INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE AND COMPUTER
-------------------------------------
Computer: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the
address bar (or the browser menu, then "Install Payday Ledger").

Android phone:
1. In the app on your computer, go to Settings and tap "Add your phone or another
   device". Copy the link and email or text it to yourself.
2. Open the link on your phone in Chrome. It connects to your Firebase project.
3. Sign in with the same email and password.
4. In Chrome's menu (three dots), tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

It works offline too. Changes you make without a connection upload when you
are back online.


MOVING DATA FROM THE CLAUDE VERSION
-----------------------------------
If you already entered things in the version inside Claude, open it there,
go to Settings, and tap "Export backup". Then in this app, Settings,
"Restore from backup", and choose that file.


UPDATING THE APP LATER
----------------------
If you get a newer version, open your payday-ledger repository on GitHub,
click "Add file", then "Upload files", drag in the new files, and commit.
Your app picks up the new version the next time it opens online.
Your budget lives in Firebase, so updating the app never touches your data.


SCHEDULE TIPS
-------------
- Every 10 days: Schedule, Add, How often: "Every X days (like every 10 days)",
  then enter 10 and pick the date of your next payment.
- Every 3 weeks, every week, the 2nd Tuesday, twice a month, yearly: all in the
  same "How often" list.
- A one-time change (a bigger bill this month, a skipped payment, paying from an
  earlier paycheck): tap that date on the Calendar or Paychecks page.
